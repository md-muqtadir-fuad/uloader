THIRD-PARTY PROGRAMS

This setup can also install three separate programs. U-loader runs them as
separate programs; they are not covered by U-loader's license above and
keep their own licenses:

  yt-dlp   finds the videos on video sites
           The Unlicense (public domain)
           https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp

  FFmpeg   joins video and audio, converts streams to MP4
           GNU LGPL version 2.1 or later
           https://ffmpeg.org  (source: see ffmpeg-build.txt)

  Deno     lets yt-dlp read YouTube in full quality
           MIT License, Copyright 2018-2026 the Deno authors
           https://github.com/denoland/deno

The full notices are in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md in the installation folder.
