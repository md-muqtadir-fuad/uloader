Privacy

U-loader and its browser extension. Last updated 22 November 2026.

In short

U-loader collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Everything stays on your computer.

The browser extension

  • It sends a link to the U-loader program on your own computer, and only when you ask: the video button, the right-click menu, the popup, or a download it catches.
  • With that link it passes the page address and the cookies of that site, so downloads that need you to be signed in still work. These go only to U-loader on your computer, and from there only to the site they belong to.
  • It notes which videos a page plays, so the popup can list them. This list stays in the browser and is cleared when you close the tab.
  • It sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.

The program

  • It connects only to the sites you download from.
  • Once a day it asks GitHub whether there is a new version (one request to api.github.com), and it runs yt-dlp's own update. You can turn both off in Settings.
  • Your settings and download list are stored in %APPDATA%\U-loader on your computer.
